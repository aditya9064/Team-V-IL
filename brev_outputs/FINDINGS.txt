Findings
- The vis (blur) control is what preserves colour; sigma_max barely matters with it.
- Lower vis weight trades colour safety for realism; below 1.0 colours start to flip.
- White balance before Transfer removes the purple cast without touching labels.
- All gate failures of the winner are side-camera images where the yellow robot arm /
  yellow table stripe is re-shaded (false alarm: no cube changed colour). Kept as strict.
- Remaining realism gap: robot-arm texture stays grainy on the side camera.
Recipe: white_balance.py -> Transfer (edge 1.0 + vis 1.0 very_low, sigma_max 80, neutral
prompt) -> transfer_qc.py gate -> only passing images go to training.
